Rung 4 of demos/cnn is built and runnable: the Zhao, Wang, Wang and Liu (2018) Section 2.1 Equation (1) multi-channel triple sum. It shows one contraction in four spellings - nested transliteration, collapsed multi-axis reduction, reduction by axis name, and im2col matmul - and proves all four bit-identical to each other (difference 0). All are checked against the native conv2d builtin as an independent oracle: exact on integer input, and 1.4e-14 on floats reported as a stated 1e-9 tolerance rather than claimed as bit-exactness. Uses the frozen [out_y, out_x, C, kh, kw] windows axis order so the kernel aligns by trailing position with no transpose. The equation is displayed with explicit summation limits and a full symbol gloss, and names Equation (1) as cross-correlation because the kernel is unflipped.
