Formula provenance is verified rather than assumed. tests/cnn-formula-provenance.mlpl runs in the default gate and checks that all six rungs carry @formula and @ascii annotations that round-trip through annotations(), that every summation states both bounds per docs/math-notation.md, that the rung implementing the paper's equation attributes it by number, and that the two rungs exceeding Equation (1) record that they do. The check has negative controls and was verified by introducing a bare summation into rung 1 and confirming a named failure. The step's original scene-IR and MathJax plan was NOT built: upstream shipped svg(text, 'equation'), which renders stacked limits to self-contained SVG with no toolchain or network, so the test asserts that renderer is used rather than a second implementation. tests/cnn-convolution.mlpl was also added to the gate, having been runnable but ungated.
