Implemented and shipped bounded manual-gradient linear fine-tuning on the deterministic two-feature task y=1+2*x1+3*x2. MLPL explicitly computes predictions, MSE, bias/weight gradients, batch updates, stopping decisions, fingerprints, and held-out evaluation without autodiff or external training. Sixty updates at learning rate 0.2 recover approximately [1.0272,1.9780,2.9762], reduce training MSE from 15.5 to 0.000281, and reduce held-out MSE from the zero-model baseline 65 to 0.00162 with predictions near [5,7,11]. Reports include all losses/gradient norms plus selected narrated points, initial/final/target parameters and fingerprints, update count, max-step stopping, attribution, and report size. Tests cover exact curve lengths, improvement, fingerprint change, excessive-rate rejection, bad parameter shape, and step/iteration/parameter/curve/output budgets.
