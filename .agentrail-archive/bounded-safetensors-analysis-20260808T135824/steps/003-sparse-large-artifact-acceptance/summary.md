Added opt-in sparse large-artifact acceptance for bounded Safetensors statistics. The harness locally creates and cleans a 1 MiB sparse tensor, scans it in 256-byte chunks, verifies deterministic results, and enforces a 16 MiB peak-RSS ceiling on Darwin/Linux. Observed 10,403,840 bytes RSS on the pinned macOS binary. The acceptance exposed recursive within-chunk stack growth, which was replaced with iterative MLPL folds.
